Online & Controls

Online Play

Rollback netcode

Deterministic simulation with rollback on prediction misses — matches stay responsive on rough connections.

Rooms & invites

Short room codes, invite links, and QR codes in the lobby. Direct WebRTC P2P when it connects, WebSocket relay when it doesn't.

Regions & ping

North America, Europe, Asia, South America, Oceania — auto-detected, with live ping and connection quality in the lobby.

Lobby chat

Persistent global lobby chat, synced with the server. Player names are sanitised.
